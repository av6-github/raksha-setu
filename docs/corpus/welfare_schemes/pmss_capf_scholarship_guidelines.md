# Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) for CAPFs & Assam Rifles

**Issuing Authority:** Welfare and Rehabilitation Board (WARB), Ministry of Home Affairs  
**Official Reference Number:** WARB/MHA/PMSS/Policy/2026  
**Official Portal:** https://scholarships.gov.in (National Scholarship Portal - NSP)  
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Verification Status:** Official Government Policy  

---

## 1. Scheme Objective
To encourage higher technical and professional education for dependent wards and widows of Central Armed Police Forces & Assam Rifles (CAPFs & AR) and State Police Personnel martyred during terror or Naxal attacks.

## 2. Scholarship Amount & Duration
- **Girls:** ₹3,000 per month (₹36,000 per academic year).
- **Boys:** ₹2,500 per month (₹30,000 per academic year).
- **Payment Method:** Annual direct benefit transfer (DBT) via PFMS into the student's individual Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- **Duration:** Paid for the full duration of the sanctioned professional degree course (1 to 5 years depending on programme).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiaries:**
   - Dependent wards and widows of serving or retired CAPFs & AR personnel (primarily Personnel Below Officer Rank - PBOR).
   - Priority 1: Wards/widows of personnel killed in action / border clashes.
   - Priority 2: Wards of personnel permanently disabled in action.
   - Priority 3: Wards/widows of personnel died in harness with causes attributable to service.
   - Priority 4: Wards of personnel disabled in service with disability attributable to service.
   - Priority 5: Wards of personnel awarded Gallantry Awards (Param Vir Chakra, Mahavir Chakra, Vir Chakra, President's Police Medal for Gallantry).
   - Priority 6: Wards of ex-CAPF/serving CAPF personnel.
2. **Academic Standards:**
   - Minimum Entry Qualification (MEQ): Must have scored **at least 60% marks** in 10+2 / Diploma / Graduation.
   - Renewal: Must pass subsequent years/semesters with at least 50% aggregate marks in the first attempt.
3. **Number of Children:** Restricted to a maximum of **two children per family**.

## 4. Eligible Courses
- **First Professional Degree Courses:** Recognised by AICTE, National Medical Commission (NMC), UGC, Bar Council of India, Pharmacy Council of India.
- **Approved Streams:** B.E., B.Tech, MBBS, BDS, B.Pharm, B.Sc Nursing, BBA, BCA, B.Ed, LLB, MBA, MCA.
- **Ineligible Courses:** Distance education, correspondence degrees, diploma programmes, and master's degrees (other than MBA/MCA).

## 5. Application Process
- Applications must be submitted through the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** (https://scholarships.gov.in).
- Verification is performed first by the head of the educational institute, followed by the respective force Headquarter / WARB nodal officer.
