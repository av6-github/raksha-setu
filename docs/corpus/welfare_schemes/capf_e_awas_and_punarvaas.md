# CAPF e-Awas Portal & CAPF Punarvaas Guidelines

**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) & Welfare and Rehabilitation Board (WARB)  
**Official Reference Number:** MHA Order No. 27012/03/2022-Police-II  
**Official Portal:** https://capfeawas.gov.in / https://punarvaas.warb-mha.gov.in  
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Verification Status:** Official Government Policy  

---

## 1. CAPF e-Awas Housing Portal
- **Objective:** Web portal launched by MHA to automate and optimize the allocation of General Pool Residential Accommodation (GPRA) and force-specific residential quarters among CAPFs and Assam Rifles personnel.
- **Inter-Force Allocation:** Personnel of one CAPF (e.g. BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB) can apply for and be allotted vacant quarters belonging to any other CAPF at locations where their own force has no vacant family quarters.
- **Eligibility:** All serving personnel of BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles, and NSG.
- **Application Method:** Serving personnel register on the portal using their force unique ID and apply online based on their station of posting.
- **Portal URL:** https://capfeawas.gov.in

## 2. CAPF Punarvaas (WARB Re-employment & Welfare)
- **Objective:** Digital employment matching platform facilitating post-retirement job opportunities in private security agencies, corporate facilities, and defense industries for retired CAPF personnel and dependent family members.
- **Eligibility:** Retired/released personnel of Central Armed Police Forces (PBOR and Officers) with exemplary or good service records.
- **Welfare Grants & Mobility Equipment:** WARB provides financial assistance for prosthetic limbs, wheelchairs, and motorized tricycles for personnel disabled during active operations.
- **Portal URL:** https://punarvaas.warb-mha.gov.in
