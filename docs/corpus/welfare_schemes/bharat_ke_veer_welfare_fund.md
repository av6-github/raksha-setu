# Bharat Ke Veer (India's Bravehearts) Trust Guidelines

**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India  
**Official Reference Number:** MHA Order No. 11026/1/2017-PMA/BKV  
**Official Portal:** https://bharatkeveer.gov.in  
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Verification Status:** Official Government Policy  

---

## 1. Objective & Mandate
Bharat Ke Veer is a trust established under the Ministry of Home Affairs to facilitate public and corporate financial contributions directly to the Next of Kin (NoK) of Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) personnel who have laid down their lives in the line of duty fighting terrorists, insurgents, or guarding international borders.

## 2. Financial Assistance Limits & Disbursements
- **Maximum Limit Per Braveheart:** **₹25 Lakh** per martyr (revised upward from ₹15 Lakh on October 10, 2023).
- **Direct Credit:** Donations made for an individual martyr are credited directly into the bank account of the designated Next of Kin (NoK).
- **General Corpus Fund:** If donations for an individual exceed ₹25 Lakh, or if contributors choose the general corpus, funds are deposited into the "Bharat Ke Veer Corpus", which is managed by a high-level committee comprising the Union Home Secretary, Director Generals of CAPFs, and eminent public trustees.
- **Minimum Guaranteed Entitlement:** The Trust guarantees that the NoK of every operational martyr receives a combined minimum total entitlement of **₹1 Crore** when taking into account Central ex-gratia, state ex-gratia, risk fund, and Bharat Ke Veer assistance.
- **Support for Parents of Married Martyrs:** As per the policy amendment effective May 1, 2020, parents of married martyrs receive an additional financial grant of **₹10 Lakh** from the Bharat Ke Veer corpus to ensure their long-term sustenance.

## 3. Covered Forces
1. Border Security Force (BSF)
2. Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)
3. Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)
4. Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)
5. Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)
6. Assam Rifles (AR)
7. National Security Guard (NSG)

## 4. Verification & Tax Exemption
- All recipient profiles and casualty details are verified and authenticated by the Directorate General of the respective force.
- Contributions qualify for 100% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.
