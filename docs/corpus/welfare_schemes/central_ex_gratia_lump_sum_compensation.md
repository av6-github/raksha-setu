# Central Ex-Gratia Lump Sum Compensation Guidelines

**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) & Ministry of Finance, Government of India  
**Official Reference Number:** MHA OM No. 27011/64/2016-R&W  
**Official Portal:** https://mha.gov.in / https://warb.gov.in  
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Verification Status:** Official Government Policy  

---

## 1. Scope & Purpose
Payment of ex-gratia lump sum compensation to the families of Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) personnel who die in the performance of their bona fide official duties under various operational circumstances.

## 2. Compensation Slabs & Categories
1. **Death in Performance of Duties (Accidents / Natural Calamities):**
   - **Amount:** ₹25 Lakh.
   - Applies to deaths occurring during election deployment, natural disaster rescue operations, official travel accidents, or high-altitude terrain hazards not involving enemy action.
2. **Death in Border Clashes, Enemy Action, or Counter-Insurgency:**
   - **Amount:** ₹35 Lakh.
   - Applies to personnel killed in active encounters with terrorists, insurgents, extremists, anti-social elements, or during border firing.
3. **Death in Enemy Action in War or War-like Engagements:**
   - **Amount:** ₹45 Lakh.
   - Applies to operational deaths occurring during designated war-like operations, high-altitude cross-border hostilities, or specific notified military engagements.

## 3. Disbursal & Beneficiaries
- Paid 100% directly to the legally nominated Next of Kin (NoK) as recorded in the service book.
- Ex-gratia lump sum compensation is paid in addition to Liberalized Family Pension (LFP) or Extraordinary Pension (EOP), Central Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme (CGEGIS), and General Provident Fund (GPF) balances.
- No income tax is deductible on ex-gratia lump sum compensation.

## 4. Processing Channel
- Claim initiated immediately by the Unit Commandant upon casualty notification.
- Sanctioned by the Ministry of Home Affairs within 30 days of receipt of completed court of inquiry and verification papers.
