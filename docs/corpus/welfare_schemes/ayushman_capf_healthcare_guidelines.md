# Ayushman CAPF Healthcare Scheme Guidelines

**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) & National Health Authority (NHA)  
**Official Reference Number:** MHA OM No. II-27011/38/2020-PF.I/II  
**Official Portal:** https://pmjay.gov.in / https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in  
**Last Verified:** 2026-09-25  
**Verification Status:** Official Government Policy  

---

## 1. Overview & Objective
The Ayushman CAPF scheme is an initiative by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and the National Health Authority (NHA) to provide 100% cashless and paperless medical treatment to serving Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) personnel and their dependent families across India.

## 2. Beneficiary Coverage
- **Eligible Forces:** All serving personnel of the 7 Central Armed Police Forces:
  1. Border Security Force (BSF)
  2. Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)
  3. Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)
  4. Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)
  5. Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)
  6. Assam Rifles (AR)
  7. National Security Guard (NSG)
  *(Also includes National Disaster Response Force - NDRF as per January 2024 order).*
- **Dependent Members:** Spouses, dependent unmarried children (sons up to age 25 or until employed/married; daughters until married/employed), and dependent parents whose monthly income from all sources does not exceed statutory limits (as verified in service records).
- **Retired Personnel:** Retired personnel are not covered under this specific scheme and are covered under CGHS/CAPF pensioner medical rules.

## 3. Cashless Hospitalization & Network
- **Empanelled Facilities:**
  - **CGHS-Empanelled Hospitals:** Cashless access for both Outpatient (OPD) and Inpatient (IPD) services.
  - **PM-JAY Empanelled Hospitals:** Cashless Inpatient (IPD) treatment, major surgeries, intensive care, and diagnostic procedures.
- **Referral Rules (Updated):** Per MHA order dated March 6, 2023, the requirement for a mandatory unit referral for dependents seeking treatment at PM-JAY empanelled hospitals has been relaxed. Serving personnel stationed at forward locations can obtain treatment with their Ayushman CAPF e-card.
- **Emergency Treatment:** In acute life-threatening medical emergencies, treatment can be availed at any private non-empanelled hospital with reimbursement admissible as per standard CGHS package rates.

## 4. Documentation Required
1. Ayushman CAPF e-Card (with active ABHA number).
2. Service Identity Card of the serving officer.
3. Aadhaar Card of the patient/dependent.
